1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes Acme Corporation's requirements for the selection,
onboarding, ongoing management, and offboarding of third-party vendors and business
partners. The objective is to ensure that risks introduced by third parties are
identified, assessed, and appropriately mitigated to protect Acme Corporation's
information assets, operational continuity, and regulatory standing.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all vendors, subcontractors, and business partners that
provide services to Acme Corporation or that are granted access to Acme Corporation
systems, networks, or data. It covers both technology vendors (including cloud
service providers and managed service providers) and non-technology vendors whose
services involve access to sensitive company information.

3. VENDOR RISK MANAGEMENT (CC9.2)

3.1 Vendor Risk Assessment and Onboarding

Prior to onboarding a new vendor, Procurement & Risk Management conducts a formal
vendor risk assessment scaled to the sensitivity of the engagement:

  (a) Risk Tiering: Vendors are assigned a risk tier (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
      based on the type of data they access, the criticality of services provided,
      and the degree of integration with Acme Corporation systems.

  (b) Security Assessment: Critical and High-tier vendors must complete a security
      questionnaire covering their information security controls, data protection
      practices, incident response capabilities, and relevant certifications. Where
      available, current SOC 2 Type II reports, ISO 27001 certificates, or equivalent
      third-party assurances are collected and reviewed.

  (c) Financial and Reputational Due Diligence: Procurement conducts background
      checks on new vendors to assess financial stability and reputational risk
      prior to contract execution.

  (d) Approval: Vendor onboarding requires written approval from the business
      sponsor, the Procurement team, and — for Critical or High-tier vendors — the
      Information Security Team.

3.2 Contractual Security Requirements

All vendor contracts must include provisions addressing:

  (a) Data Protection: Requirements for vendors to protect Acme Corporation data in
      accordance with applicable law and Acme Corporation's data classification
      standards. This includes encryption of data in transit and at rest, restrictions
      on subprocessor use, and data deletion upon contract termination.

  (b) Security Incident Notification: Vendors must notify Acme Corporation's
      designated contact within 24 hours of discovering a security incident or
      suspected breach involving Acme Corporation data.

  (c) Audit Rights: Acme Corporation reserves the right to audit vendor security
      controls or require evidence of compliance with contractual security obligations
      on reasonable notice.

  (d) Right to Terminate: Contracts must include provisions allowing Acme Corporation
      to terminate the relationship without penalty in the event of a material
      security breach or sustained non-compliance.

3.3 Ongoing Vendor Monitoring

Following onboarding, vendors are subject to ongoing performance and risk monitoring:

  (a) Periodic Review: Critical and High-tier vendors are reviewed annually; Medium-
      tier vendors are reviewed every 18 months. Reviews assess current SOC reports
      or equivalent security certifications, security questionnaire updates, and any
      known incidents or breaches.

  (b) Service Level Monitoring: Procurement monitors vendor performance against
      contractually agreed service levels and escalates sustained underperformance to
      vendor account management.

  (c) Incident Tracking: Security incidents reported by vendors are logged in the
      incident tracking system and evaluated by the Information Security Team to
      assess impact on Acme Corporation systems or data.

3.4 Vendor Offboarding

When a vendor relationship is terminated, the following steps are completed:

  (a) Access Revocation: All logical and physical access granted to the vendor is
      revoked within one (1) business day of the contract end date or earlier notice
      of termination.

  (b) Data Return and Deletion: The vendor is required to return or certifiably
      destroy all Acme Corporation data within 30 days of contract termination, with
      written confirmation provided to Procurement.

  (c) Final Assessment: A closing review is documented, including any open issues,
      lessons learned, and confirmation that contractual wind-down obligations have
      been satisfied.

4. VENDOR ACCESS TO SYSTEMS

Vendors granted access to Acme Corporation systems are subject to the same access
control requirements applicable to internal users, including unique account
credentials, least-privilege access, and multi-factor authentication where required.
Vendor access is reviewed as part of the periodic vendor review cycle.

5. RESPONSIBILITIES

  - Procurement & Risk Management: Owns and administers this policy; manages vendor
    onboarding, contract review, and ongoing monitoring activities.
  - Information Security Team: Conducts security assessments for Critical and High-
    tier vendors; reviews vendor incident reports; approves security contract terms.
  - Business Sponsors: Identify vendor requirements; serve as primary relationship
    contacts; participate in vendor performance reviews.
  - Legal: Reviews and approves contractual terms; advises on data protection
    obligations and breach notification requirements.

6. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to vendor risk assessment requirements must be approved by both the
Procurement lead and the Information Security Team and documented with a defined
remediation plan and timeline.
