1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the requirements and controls governing logical access to
Acme Corporation's information systems, applications, and data. The intent of this
policy is to ensure that access to information assets is granted only to authorized
individuals based on legitimate business need, and that access controls are designed
to prevent, detect, and deter unauthorized access.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers,
and third-party service providers who access Acme Corporation systems. It covers all
information systems owned or operated by Acme Corporation, including on-premises
infrastructure, cloud-hosted environments, and software-as-a-service (SaaS)
applications.

3. POLICY STATEMENTS

3.1 Logical Access Controls (CC6.1)

Acme Corporation implements logical access security controls across all systems and
infrastructure to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of
information assets. These controls include:

  (a) Identification and Authentication: All users must be uniquely identified prior
      to accessing any Acme Corporation system. Shared or generic accounts are
      prohibited except where technically necessary, and such exceptions require
      documented approval from the Information Security Team and department head.

  (b) Access Restriction: User access privileges are restricted to the minimum
      necessary to perform assigned job functions (principle of least privilege).
      Access to sensitive systems, administrative interfaces, and production
      environments is granted based on documented business justification and manager
      authorization.

  (c) Segregation of Access: Access privileges are structured to enforce separation
      of duties. No single individual shall have access rights that allow them to
      both initiate and approve a transaction, or to access both production systems
      and source code repositories without documented compensating controls.

  (d) Prevention and Detection of Unauthorized Access: Access control lists, network
      segmentation, and host-based access controls are deployed across all system
      tiers. Authentication failures, privilege escalations, and anomalous access
      patterns are logged and monitored by the Security Operations function.

  (e) Physical and Remote Access: Remote access to internal systems is permitted only
      via the corporate VPN using approved configurations. Direct remote desktop or
      SSH access to production servers requires individual accountability and is
      restricted to members of the Infrastructure and Security teams.

3.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (CC6.6)

Acme Corporation requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) to protect against
unauthorized access from sources external to its network boundaries.

  (a) MFA Requirement: Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for:
      - All remote access to corporate systems via VPN
      - Access to cloud management consoles (AWS, Azure, GCP)
      - Access to privileged or administrative accounts
      - Access to systems containing personally identifiable information (PII) or
        payment card data
      - All SaaS applications processing sensitive business data

  (b) Approved MFA Methods: Acceptable second factors include hardware security keys
      (FIDO2/WebAuthn), authenticator applications (TOTP-based), and push
      notifications through approved enterprise authentication platforms. SMS-based
      one-time passwords are deprecated and must not be used for high-risk system
      access. Exceptions require written approval from the CISO.

  (c) Boundary Protection: In addition to MFA, Acme Corporation deploys perimeter
      security controls including next-generation firewalls, intrusion detection
      systems (IDS), and web application firewalls (WAF) to protect against threats
      originating outside the entity's system boundaries. All external-facing
      endpoints are subject to continuous vulnerability scanning.

  (d) Credential Protection: Authentication credentials must not be shared, embedded
      in code or configuration files, or transmitted in cleartext. Secrets management
      solutions are used to store and rotate service account credentials.

3.3 User Provisioning and Access Management (CC6.2)

Access provisioning follows a defined workflow to ensure appropriate authorization
before credentials are issued. When a new employee or contractor requires system
access, the hiring manager submits an access request through the IT service desk.
Access is provisioned following approval; however, the formal registration process
and explicit authorization steps are managed at the departmental level and may vary
by business unit.

Role-based access control (RBAC) templates have been defined for common job
functions to facilitate consistent provisioning. Access reviews are conducted
periodically to validate continued business need.

3.4 Access Termination

Upon separation or role change, the People Operations team notifies IT of required
access modifications. Account deprovisioning for terminated employees is completed
within one (1) business day of the effective termination date. Contractor and
third-party access is revoked upon contract expiration or earlier termination.

3.5 Access Reviews

Access rights for all users are reviewed on a semi-annual basis. System owners are
responsible for certifying the continued appropriateness of user access within their
systems. Certification results are documented and exceptions are remediated within
30 days.

3.6 Access Changes and Change Procedures (CC8.1)

Significant changes to access control configurations — including modifications to
role definitions, privileged account permissions, or firewall rules — are subject to
the change management process. Such changes require documented rationale and
appropriate approvals prior to implementation in production environments.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

  - Information Security Team: Maintains this policy, operates access control
    tooling, reviews access logs, and manages exception processes.
  - IT Operations: Implements provisioning and deprovisioning requests; maintains
    identity management infrastructure.
  - Managers and System Owners: Authorize access requests; certify access during
    periodic reviews.
  - All Personnel: Protect their credentials, report suspected unauthorized access,
    and comply with all requirements of this policy.

5. EXCEPTIONS

Requests for exception to this policy must be submitted in writing to the
Information Security Team and include a description of the business need, proposed
compensating controls, and a defined expiration date. Approved exceptions are logged
and reviewed quarterly.

6. ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination of employment or contract. Legal proceedings may be initiated where
applicable law has been violated.
